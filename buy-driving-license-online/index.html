Questions

Before you send an enquiry

Can I start the enquiry online?

Yes. Send your preferred licence class, current location and available dates. The detailed training plan is then discussed personally.

Can training in Poland be completed without travelling?

No. Regular driving-school training includes in-person lessons and examination appointments. Only the initial organisational enquiry can be completed online.

Which costs should I compare?

Compare training, driving lessons, fees, travel, accommodation and reasonable time reserves. Ask which items are included before deciding.
